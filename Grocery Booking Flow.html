One order, from the moment a customer starts shopping to a confirmed booking with an approved payment. Each lane is one part of the system. Arrows show what happens next, and the pills are the events the services send each other.

Status names along the way

Order

  1. PENDING
  2. CONFIRMED
  3. ACCEPTED
  4. PACKED
  5. READY_FOR_PICKUP
  6. OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
  7. DELIVERED

Can also end as CANCELLED REJECTED DELIVERY_FAILED Cancelling is possible until the partner picks the order up.

Payment, online

  1. PENDING
  2. AWAITING_PAYMENT
  3. PAID

FAILED declined, or not paid within 15 minutes. REFUNDED paid, then cancelled, rejected or not delivered.

Payment, cash on delivery

  1. PENDING
  2. CASH_ON_DELIVERY
  3. COLLECTED

CANCELLED the order ended before the cash was collected.

Drawn from this repository: OrderStatus (order-service), PaymentStatus (payment-service), the order saga in docs/event-catalog.md, and the cart-service, order-service and payment-service READMEs. Amounts are the platform defaults: ₹30 delivery fee, free from ₹299, minimum order ₹99.
